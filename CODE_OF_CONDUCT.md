# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, collaborative community. Participate in good
faith, welcome different perspectives, and focus discussion on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and sharing private or
confidential information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that
do not follow this code of conduct. Report conduct concerns through the private
security-reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical.
